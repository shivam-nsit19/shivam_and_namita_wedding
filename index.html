Our Story

We’re quite different in the best possible way. Shivam is the easy-going, “take a chill pill” kind of person. Namita, on the other hand, a solid planner and occasionally hits the panic button. Somehow, we balance each other perfectly and just make sense together.

The best part? Neither of us was looking for love when we met.

Shivam had simply come to meet an old school friend. Namita, meanwhile, was recovering from a terrible bout of food poisoning and, in all honesty, should have stayed home. But for reasons we’ll never understand, she showed up anyway in a low-effort T-shirt, jeans, and absolutely no intention of impressing anyone.

As they say, no matter how you feel, just dress up and show up. Turns out, that’s some pretty solid advice.

One meeting. A conversation that somehow revolved around nausea and puking. A few laughs. And before we knew it, we had found something neither of us was expecting.

Who would’ve thought destiny had such a beautiful love story in store for us?

And now, here we are ready to begin our forever as husband and wife. We can’t wait to celebrate this special day with all of you.
